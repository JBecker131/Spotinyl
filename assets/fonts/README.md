# Fonts

**Memory of 2018** — the Spotinyl wordmark face.

- Designer: Khurasan (<https://www.khurasan.net>)
- Source: <https://www.fontspace.com/memory-of-2018-font-f31569>
- Licence: freeware, free for personal and commercial use.

Only the OpenType cut is shipped; the TrueType cut from the download adds
~20 KB for no benefit in Chrome.

**Blue Ridge** — the playback-clock face in the popup.

- Designer: Eko Bimantara (<https://ekobimantara.com>)
- Source: <https://www.fontspace.com/blue-ridge-font-f168771>
- Licence: freeware, free for personal and commercial use.

Its digits are proportional rather than tabular, so `font-variant-numeric`
has nothing to select; the elapsed reading shifts by a pixel or two as the
digits change.
